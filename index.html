Scoring method · v1

Answered symptom items map to 0 (none), 1 (mild), 2 (moderate), or 3 (severe), averaged and scaled to 0–100. Poor sleep restoration and low food/hydration intake are scored as burden. Bristol type, bowel frequency, sleep duration and manual assistance remain separate because their meaning requires context. This is an unvalidated personal tracking index, not medical advice.
